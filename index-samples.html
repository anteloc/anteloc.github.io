LDraw Nova Outputs

About

The images and LDraw models in this gallery are by-products of agents generating LDraw models. They are not a curated showcase of finished work. They include, for example:

  • full official models, selected as examples from the LDraw Official Model Repository
  • submodels extracted from official models
  • attempts at modifying and/or repairing assemblies
  • trial-and-error iterations, tests and other work in progress

Click an image to view it full size, or use the link below it to download the corresponding .mpd model.
